# Release 1.7.10

**Date:** 2026-10-23
**Previous:** v1.7.9

## Highlights

Fixes the opt-in settings notice that couldn't be dismissed and kept coming back
(https://wordpress.org/support/topic/settings-notice-wont-go-away/), by updating the bundled opt-in library to 1.3.2.

## What's New

### Fixes
- The settings notice could not be dismissed and came back on every page load.
- On sites running other Fullworks free plugins, the opt-in notice and menu could belong to the wrong plugin.
- Leaving the opt-in screen without choosing left the notice up for good. Visiting settings now shows the opt-in screen again until you click Allow or Skip.

### Improvements
- The notice links straight to the opt-in screen, with accurate wording.
- Bundled opt-in library updated to 1.3.2.

## Breaking Changes

None.

## Migration Guide

None. Stored opt-in choices (`SUE_form_rendered`) and dismissals (`SUE_notice_dismissed`) are kept.
